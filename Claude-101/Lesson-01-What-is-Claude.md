### 📌 What Is This About?

This lesson is the starting point of the whole course. Simply put — before you start driving the car, this lesson shows you what is under the bonnet.

### 💡 Why Does This Matter?

Many people think Claude is "just a chatbot" — something you ask a question to and it replies. That thinking limits how much value you can get from it. Claude is built to be a **thinking partner**, not just a question-answering machine. Once you understand that difference, you start using Claude for bigger things — planning, analysing, writing, coding — instead of only small one-off queries.

---

### 📖 The Explanation

#### What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant designed to work *with* you, like a thinking partner — not just a tool that spits out answers. Think of it like a very well-read, very quick colleague who is always available, and who brings intelligence to the table, while **you** bring the context, the real-world details, and the expertise that makes the final output meaningful.

#### The principles behind Claude

Claude is built to be **helpful, harmless, and honest**. This comes from an approach called **Constitutional AI**:

- It avoids toxic or discriminatory outputs.
- It avoids helping with illegal or unethical activities.
- It is trained to align with human values and to operate transparently — no hidden agenda.

#### Claude is more than a chatbot

A simple chatbot answers a question and stops there. Claude is capable of a much wider variety of conversational and text-processing tasks, and it does this reliably and predictably:

- Summarisation
- Search
- Creative and collaborative writing
- Question and answer
- Coding
- ...and more

Think of Claude as a thought partner who can help you tackle complex problems and work through tricky, messy situations — not just answer simple one-line questions.

#### Claude is steerable and collaborative

Claude can take direction on **personality, tone, and behaviour**. That means you can shape *how* Claude talks to you. Because of this steerability:

- It is much less likely to produce harmful outputs.
- It is easier to have a natural conversation with.
- You get your desired output with less effort, because Claude adapts to your instructions quickly.

#### You can access Claude wherever you work

Claude apps are available on all plan types — **Free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise**. Your conversations, projects, memory, and preferences all sync across devices once you are signed in. Whether you are at your office desk or on the move, Claude is available through **web, desktop, and mobile apps**.

#### Understanding Claude's capabilities

Claude helps with a lot more than simple question-and-answer chat. It works like an assistant that can both **automate** (do the work for you) and **augment** (make your own work better). Some big strengths:

- **Writing and content creation** — social media posts, professional emails, complex reports. Since Claude takes direction on personality and tone, you can go back and forth with it until the writing sounds exactly like "you."
- **Research and analysis** — Claude helps explore research angles, compile findings, and analyse data to find meaningful insights. You can upload documents, and because of Claude's large **context window** (200K+ tokens — roughly 500 pages of text or more, going up to 1M tokens on Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans with supported models), Claude can consider a lot of material in one single conversation.
- **Coding assistance** — this is one of Claude's biggest strengths. It writes, debugs, and explains code across many programming languages.
- **Problem-solving and reasoning** — complex thinking tasks, maths problems, strategic analysis, and research. The newer Opus and Sonnet models are **hybrid models** — they offer near-instant responses for quick things, and an **extended thinking** mode where Claude works through a problem step by step for deeper reasoning.
- **Learning new things** — whether you are picking up a new skill or exploring an unfamiliar area, Claude adapts to your pace and style. There is also a **Learning mode**, which guides your own reasoning process instead of just giving you the answer directly — this helps build your critical thinking.

> **Good to know:** Anthropic has a use-case gallery on claude.com for inspiration by role, and a separate "AI Capabilities" course if you want a deeper dive into what AI can and cannot do.

#### Ways to access Claude

Remember — Claude is the *intelligence*. That same intelligence is available through several different "front doors," each suited to a different kind of work:

- **Claude.ai** (plus its mobile and desktop apps) — the main way most people talk to Claude: asking questions, brainstorming, creating/editing documents, research, analysis. This is the main focus of this course.
- **Claude Code** — an agentic coding tool for developers, but it can also be used for general file manipulation on your desktop. It can directly edit files, run commands, and create commits.
- **@Claude** — brings Claude directly into Slack. You can chat with Claude from the AI assistant header in any channel, or simply tag @Claude in a thread. Once Slack is connected, Claude can search your workspace's channels, DMs, and shared files for context.
- **Claude Design** — a dedicated space for turning ideas into working interfaces. Describe what you want (or start from a sketch/screenshot), and Claude builds an interactive prototype you can refine and hand off.
- **Claude for Microsoft 365** — brings Claude into Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Outlook as a sidebar, so you can analyse, draft, and edit right inside the document you already have open, carrying context from one app to the next.

### 📊 Key Concepts Snapshot

| Term | Meaning | Why It Matters |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| **Constitutional AI** | The approach that trains Claude to be helpful, harmless, and honest | Keeps Claude's behaviour aligned with human values and transparent |
| **Steerability** | Claude's ability to take direction on tone, personality, behaviour | Lets you shape how Claude talks with less back-and-forth effort |
| **Context window** | The amount of text Claude can "hold in mind" at once (200K+ tokens, up to 1M on some plans) | Lets Claude work with very large documents in a single chat |
| **Hybrid models (Opus/Sonnet)** | Models that can respond instantly OR use extended thinking | Gives you a choice between speed and deep reasoning |
| **Learning mode** | A mode that guides your thinking instead of giving direct answers | Helps you build skills, not just get answers |
| **Claude.ai / Code / @Claude / Design / Microsoft 365** | The different "doors" into the same Claude intelligence | Lets you use Claude exactly where your work already happens |

### 🏢 Real-World Scenario

**Situation:** Your manager tells you, "We are giving the team access to Claude — see how it can help."

**Without understanding this lesson:** You treat Claude like a search engine — one-line questions, one-line answers, and you conclude "it's just a fancier Google."

**With this lesson's understanding:** You realise Claude is a thinking partner. You start giving it your role and context ("I am preparing a client report..."), let it help draft, analyse a spreadsheet you upload, and even use extended thinking for a tricky strategic problem. You also know it is available through Slack (@Claude) and inside Word/Excel — so you don't restrict yourself to only the web chat.
