# Lesson 11 — Claude in Action: Use-Cases by Role

> **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

---

### 📌 What Is This About?

This is a short, practical lesson — a tour of real use-cases for Claude.ai, organised by job role, so you can immediately see how everything from earlier lessons applies to *your* actual work.

---

### 💡 Why Does This Matter?

Learning concepts is good, but seeing concrete examples by role makes it click. This lesson is meant to be a launchpad — pick one or two ideas below and try them this week, rather than waiting for the "perfect" use case to appear.

---

### 📖 The Explanation

No matter what you do, Claude can help streamline your work. Each use case below links (in the original course) to a detailed guide in the Use Case Gallery with step-by-step instructions.

#### General professional use
These apply across many roles and industries:
- **Generate project status reports** — keep stakeholders informed with clear, consistent updates.
- **Analyze patterns in user feedback** — extract insights from customer comments and survey responses.
- **Package your brand guidelines in a skill** — create a reusable Claude skill that applies your brand standards.

#### Sales
Accelerate deal preparation, create compelling materials, and stay on top of competitive intelligence:
- **Build a battle card library** — competitive intelligence resources that help your team win deals.
- **Prepare for sales deals** — research prospects and organise your talking points before important meetings.
- **Create sales reports** — turn your pipeline data into clear, actionable reports.

#### Marketing
Analyse performance data and efficiently repurpose content across channels:
- **Analyze campaign performance** — extract insights from campaign metrics to inform your strategy.
- **Adapt content across platforms** — efficiently repurpose content for different channels and audiences.

#### Finance
Build models, draft documents, and make sense of complex spreadsheets:
- **Build financial models** — create and refine financial projections with Claude's help.
- **Draft investment memos** — structure and write investment analyses more efficiently.
- **Understand and extend an inherited spreadsheet** — decode complex spreadsheets and add new functionality.

#### HR
Create better onboarding experiences and documentation:
- **Create new hire onboarding guides** — develop comprehensive onboarding materials tailored to different roles.

#### Legal
Track complex timelines and manage discovery processes:
- **Track discovery timelines and analyze patterns** — organise case timelines and identify key patterns in legal documents.

#### Research
Plan literature reviews and verify data analysis:
- **Plan your literature review** — organise your approach to reviewing academic sources.
- **Verify statistics from raw data** — double-check calculations and statistical analyses.

#### Explore more

These examples are just the beginning. The full **Use Case Gallery** (claude.com/resources/use-cases) has the entire collection for inspiration, whatever your role.

### 🏢 Real-World Scenario

**Situation:** You're in Finance and just inherited a multi-tab spreadsheet model from a colleague who has left the company.

**How this lesson applies:** Instead of clicking through every tab trying to piece it together yourself, you use the "Understand and extend an inherited spreadsheet" use case — ask Claude to walk you through what each tab does and how they connect, before you make any changes. This turns a task that could take a full day into something you understand in minutes.
